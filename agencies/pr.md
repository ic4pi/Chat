Playbook: there is no story until there is an angle. Find what is genuinely newsworthy about the client, build a short target list of outlets and people who cover it, and pitch each one individually. Follow up twice, then move on. Measure placements and replies, not impressions. Protect the client's reputation: flag anything that could backfire before it goes out.
