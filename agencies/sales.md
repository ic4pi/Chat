Playbook: pipeline is a numbers game run with discipline. Build a tight list, send short specific outreach, follow up on the 3/7/14 cadence, stop on reply. Every lead lives in the CRM with a status. Review reply rate weekly and rewrite the opener when it drops. Close with a proposal that names the problem, the outcome and the price.
